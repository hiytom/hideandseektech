Privacy Policy

Shenzhen Hide and Seek Technology Co., Ltd. operates consumer product businesses through online retail channels. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through this website and our internal business operations.

Information we collect

We may collect business contact information when someone emails us. For internal store operations, we may process account, catalog, inventory, order, performance, and operational log data from our own retail accounts.

How we use information

We use information to operate our own stores, maintain product listings, plan inventory, respond to customer support issues, review business performance, secure account access, and improve internal workflows.

Information sharing

We do not sell or rent business data. We may share limited information with service providers only when needed for hosting, infrastructure, logistics, customer support, accounting, security, or legal compliance.

Account and buyer data

Access to marketplace account data is limited to authorized internal users and systems. Buyer information, when available through retail channels, is used only for order support, customer service, and compliance with platform policies.

Data retention

We retain business records, operational reports, and logs only as long as needed for store operations, troubleshooting, accounting, security, and compliance.

Security

We use access controls, credential separation, least-privilege permissions, transport encryption, and logging to protect business data. More detail is available on our Data Security page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact admin@hideandseektech.com.
