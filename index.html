Purpose

Built for our own Amazon seller advertising account

Internal use only

Shenzhen Hide and Seek Technology Co., Ltd. does not operate as an agency, marketplace, or third-party advertising platform. The tools described on this site are used to support our own seller advertising operations.

Operational automation

Our software helps retrieve advertising reports, monitor campaign performance, identify inefficient spend, and apply controlled campaign changes based on pre-defined business rules.

Human oversight

Optimization rules are bounded by budget limits, bid-change limits, audit logs, and review workflows. The system is designed to reduce repetitive manual work while preserving operational control.

Data Use

Advertising data is used only for account management

Data we may access

  • Advertising profiles and account identifiers
  • Campaign, ad group, keyword, and targeting configuration
  • Performance reports such as impressions, clicks, spend, sales, ACOS, ROAS, and conversion data
  • Search term, advertised product, and targeting reports used for optimization decisions

Data we do not sell or share

  • We do not sell advertising data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data to serve third-party advertisers.
  • We do not request buyer personal information for advertising automation.
  • We do not provide public access to advertising credentials or reports.
